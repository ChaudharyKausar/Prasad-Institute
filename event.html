Events & Activities

2024

Career Guidance Seminar

At Prasad Institute, we regularly organize a variety of educational and career-focused events to empower our students. These events are thoughtfully designed to inspire learning, build confidence, and provide practical guidance for a successful future.

Our Events Include:
  • Career Guidance Seminars – Helping students explore the right career options with expert advice.
  • Skill Development Workshops – Enhancing essential skills and preparing for real-world challenges.
  • Motivational Talks & Expert Sessions – Learning from achievers and industry professionals.

These programs offer valuable insights and opportunities to interact with experienced mentors and counselors. We believe that such events play a crucial role in shaping well-rounded individuals and preparing them for success.

2025

Combined Parent Meeting

At Prasad Institute, we believe in building strong partnerships with parents to support every child’s growth. Our Combined Parent Meetings are organized to keep parents informed about academic progress, overall development, and future goals. These sessions create an open platform for meaningful discussions between teachers, parents, and students.

What Happens in the Meeting:
  • Detailed review of student performance and attendance
  • Feedback and suggestions from teachers
  • Guidance on how parents can support learning at home
  • Discussion on upcoming academic plans and activities
Why Attend:
  • Understand your child’s strengths and areas of improvement
  • Clarify doubts and set clear goals together
  • Strengthen collaboration between parents and faculty
2025

Admission Counseling

At Prasad Institute, we regularly conduct Admission Counseling sessions to help students and parents make informed decisions about their education. These sessions are designed to provide complete clarity on available courses, eligibility requirements, and the step-by-step admission process.

Our expert counselors guide you at every stage, from understanding career prospects to filling out application forms accurately. With personalized advice, you can confidently plan your academic journey and choose the right path for your future success.

Highlights of the Counseling Session:
  • Personalized course and career guidance
  • Complete information on eligibility and admission steps
  • Assistance with documentation and application forms
  • One-on-one doubt clearing with experienced counselors

Class Anniversary Celebration

At Prasad Institute, we celebrate the Class Anniversary to honor the journey, achievements, and memories shared by every batch. This special occasion strengthens bonds among students, teachers, and parents while recognizing milestones reached together.

The celebration creates a positive environment where everyone comes together to reflect on progress, share experiences, and inspire the next phase of learning and growth. It’s a proud moment that builds a sense of belonging and motivation.

Highlights:
  • Felicitation of outstanding performers
  • Sharing success stories and memories
  • Fun activities, cultural performances, and games
  • Interaction with faculty and fellow students

Motivational Movie Break

At Prasad Institute, we organize Motivational Movie Breaks to inspire students with powerful stories and real-life examples of determination, courage, and success. Watching such films together helps students relax, reflect, and recharge their minds in a positive environment.

These sessions are designed to combine learning with entertainment, encouraging students to adopt valuable life lessons and stay motivated to achieve their goals. It’s a refreshing break that leaves a lasting impact.

Highlights of the Movie Break:
  • Screening of inspiring movies and documentaries
  • Group discussions to share thoughts and learnings
  • Fun and interactive activities after the movie
  • Creating a positive and motivating atmosphere
